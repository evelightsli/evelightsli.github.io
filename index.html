Creating an experience

Eve Lights grew out of more than 15 years in high-end commercial and experiential lighting design. We bring that same attention to detail to every home we light, so you get a display that looks finished from the curb and from the front door.

Respect for your home comes first. We use the highest-grade materials and tools available, and we fabricate our own mounting hardware in house.

  • Custom clips and mountsMade in our shop to fit your shingles, gutters and trim.
  • Zero holes left behindNo nails, staples or screws. Your house comes down exactly as it went up.
  • Neat wire managementWire cut to length and tucked out of sight, so you see light, not cords.

What we light

Every display is designed for the property in front of us. Here is where we usually start.

Residential holiday lighting

Clean, continuous lines that trace the shape of your home and make it read beautifully at night.

  • Rooflines, gutters and ridge lines
  • Peaks, dormers and architectural framing
  • Windows, columns and entryways

Landscape and tree lighting

Trees and greenery wrapped trunk to tip with tight, even spacing, so they glow from within instead of looking draped.

  • Trunk and branch wrapping
  • Shrubs, hedges and garden beds
  • Walkway and driveway lighting

Commercial and experiential lighting

For storefronts, restaurants, offices and properties that want guests to feel the season the moment they arrive. This is where our commercial background shows.

  • Facades and storefront framing
  • Courtyards, entrances and trees
  • Installation scheduled around your hours

Questions we hear most

Don't see yours? Call 631-697-5012 and we'll answer it.

What happens if a bulb goes out?

Call or text us. We'll have it fixed within 48 hours, often the same day. Maintenance is included for the whole season at no extra cost.

Is the price really all-inclusive?

Yes. Your quote covers design, installation, season-long maintenance, removal and off-season storage. There are no surprise fees later.

Do I own the lights?

Your season price covers the lights along with everything we do with them. We label and store your display between seasons, so it's ready to go back up next year. Ask us about the details when we quote.

When do you take the lights down?

After the holidays, on a date we schedule with you, usually in January. You don't have to call to remind us.

Will installation damage my roof or gutters?

No. We use custom clips and mounts made in our shop, with no nails, staples or screws. When the lights come down, there's nothing to patch.

When should I book?

As early as you can. Install dates fill up as the season gets closer, and early booking gives you the widest choice.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Eve Lights, LLC is licensed and insured, and our technicians are trained to work safely at height.
